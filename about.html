About our kitchen

Rooted in family recipes, built for modern cravings.

Lea Tapdasan began with a simple belief: every dish should taste like home, celebrate heritage, and still feel special enough for a weekend treat. From a small kitchen experiment to a growing culinary brand in Mandaue City, Cebu, that philosophy remains the heart of everything we serve.

We honor the rich flavors of Japanese, Chinese, and Filipino cuisine by blending traditional techniques with modern plating, comforting ingredients, and clean, honest service.
